Meta Ads

Efficient and Holding

Meta consolidated last period's turnaround rather than giving it back: 83 leads at $113 each on 7% less spend. Cost per lead has now fallen from $228 to $127 to $113 across three reports. Brochure Download is the standout — 24 leads at $51, driven entirely by one static building image with no video at all. Remarketing delivered 8 at $58. Website & Organic

Traffic Up, Quality Flat

Site traffic jumped to 9,400 users, up 49% — but read it carefully. The broker toolkit again holds visitors longest, over nine minutes.
