Executive Summary

Record Month

July closed as the strongest month of the year: $21.1K in paid media produced 107 leads at $197 each, holding the record pace at the best unit economics to date. Meta delivered 78 leads at $116, and Google search converted 28 leads at a 1.48% search-only rate — its best reading this year — with Branded Search at $54 per conversion. The phone recovered without fully healing: 17 calls, up 89% on June, at a 71% answer rate — improved from 67%, still short of the 85% target. Site traffic nearly doubled month-over-month, but most of the increase is Display reach and unmeasured channels: Display spent $2,682 on 1.33M impressions for one tracked conversion, Microsoft/Bing grew to 916 sessions with no cost data, and a new WSJ summer-ads channel appeared. Meanwhile the pipeline rebuilt — the warm bench rose from 19 to 24 with 12 new named prospects, 8 sales visits or meetings, and 2 reservations holding.

Google Ads

Search Best of Year · Display Dilutes

Search delivered its best month of the year. On a search-only basis the account converted 28 leads at a 1.48% conversion rate and roughly $332 each, with website form fills up 11% to 31 and phone-call leads up to 5. Branded Search converted 8 times at a 43.9% click-through and $54 per lead; Non-Brand Sarasota stayed the volume engine at 18.5 conversions. The blended conversion rate reads 0.65% only because Display carried 1.33 million impressions and 2,532 clicks for a single tracked conversion. Buyers skew 45–64 and mobile-first (67.5% of conversions), and 79% of converters are non-agents — genuine prospective buyers.

Website & Organic

Traffic Doubles · Attribution Gaps

Site visitors nearly doubled month-over-month to 9.7K (+93% vs June) with views up 96% to 17.5K — but most of that surge is Display reach, and pages per user held at 1.8, so the added volume is awareness-stage rather than deeper engagement. Organic moved the right way: average position improved to 13.7 (+5.2% vs June), impressions grew 12% to 10K, and queries reached 324. The attribution gaps are the story to fix: Microsoft/Bing grew to 916 sessions with no cost data in the dashboard, a new WSJ summer-ads channel appeared at 474 sessions, and roughly 960 impressions still leak to Miramar, FL apartment searches that never click.
